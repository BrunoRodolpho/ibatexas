NEW-P1-CPF — CPF regex misses CPF-followed-by-`-` — AFTER
==========================================================

Date: 2026-05-23
Fix file: packages/llm-provider/src/audit-redactor.ts:233
Test file: packages/llm-provider/src/__tests__/audit-redactor.test.ts
           (5 new tests in "regex defense" describe block)

Fix
---
Lookahead changed:
  from: (?![\d.-])
  to:   (?!\d)

The previous lookahead rejected `-` and `.` as trailing chars, so a
CPF concatenated with a hyphen-prefixed tag (`12345678900-foo`)
slipped past. The new lookahead only blocks ANOTHER DIGIT (meaning
we're inside a longer number). `-` and `.` are now treated as
non-CPF boundary characters and the match fires.

Lookbehind unchanged: a `-` BEFORE the CPF would still mean we're
mid-sequence in a longer number (`999-12345678900-456` is a long
hyphenated number, not a CPF).
