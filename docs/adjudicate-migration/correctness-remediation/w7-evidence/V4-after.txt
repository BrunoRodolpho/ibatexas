── Why inline migration was rejected ────────────────────────────────────────

The 10 newly-surfaced sites are LLM-callable cart-store mutations.
Inline migration requires:
  (a) New `medusa.store.cart.*` intent kinds added to
      MEDUSA_INTENT_KINDS in packages/tools/src/medusa/adjudicated.ts.
  (b) Per-kind policy bundle decisions (auth/taint/business guards).
  (c) Test coverage for the LLM-flow → kernel → SDK dispatch chain.

That's a multi-day W9-scope task (cart-egress governance), not a
30-minute V4 scan-widening closure. Deferring to W9 with explicit
machine-tracked allowlist is the right trade — V4 ships the gate-
hardening benefit (no NEW regressions can slip into packages/tools/src/)
without conflating the cart-egress migration into the wave.
